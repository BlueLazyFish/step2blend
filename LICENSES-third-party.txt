================================================================================
Open CASCADE Technology (OCCT)
================================================================================

Step 2 Blend dynamically links to Open CASCADE Technology version 7.9.x.
The OCCT runtime libraries are bundled inside the addon's lib/ folder
(libTK*.dylib on macOS, TK*.dll on Windows) and loaded at runtime.

Source:    https://dev.opencascade.org
Licence:   GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1, with the
           Open CASCADE additional exception (see below).

The OCCT licence text:
    https://dev.opencascade.org/sites/default/files/OCCT_LGPL_EXCEPTION_1.0.txt

Per the LGPL, you have the right to relink Step 2 Blend against a
different (compatible) build of OCCT. The bundled libraries can be
replaced with substitutes you build yourself from OCCT source.

The OCCT source is unmodified; no patches have been applied.
